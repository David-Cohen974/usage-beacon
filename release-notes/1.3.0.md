UsageBeacon 1.3.0 brings a cleaner interface and more reliable budget tracking.

- A scrollable menu with readable amounts, persistent controls, and simpler provider rows.
- Organized Settings pages with native controls and widget-inspired gradients in light and dark mode.
- More reliable widget refreshes through an atomic shared snapshot.
- Workday budgets recalculate when schedules or calendars change, without waiting for a provider request.
- Choose whether a calendar excludes busy all-day events, all-day time off, or Israel full holidays only. Minor fasts and holiday eves remain workdays under the full-holiday rule; overlapping weekends and leave are not deducted twice.
- Providers refresh independently, and failed refreshes retain the last successful timestamp.

Existing calendar selections retain their previous rule. Review Settings → Workdays & Calendars to choose the appropriate policy for each calendar.
