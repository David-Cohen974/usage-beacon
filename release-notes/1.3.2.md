# UsageBeacon 1.3.2

- Click anywhere on a provider's header row to expand or collapse its settings. Sync and connection actions are inside the expanded panel.
- The first provider to successfully sync is selected for the menu bar meter automatically. Existing selections and subsequent manual opt-outs are preserved.
- Switches keep a blue on-state when the window is inactive, with a checkmark and thumb position to distinguish on from off. Disabled states, keyboard access, VoiceOver, and reduced-motion settings are retained.
